Origin Story

Beginning
of BeachSports

Founded in 1995 by LA County Lifeguards, BeachSports began as a youth beach and surf camp in the South Bay, created to give kids structured, safe education in ocean awareness and beach skills.

In 2010, Tommy Ostendorf reprogrammed and evolved the camp, keeping its core mission intact while shaping the programs into what BeachSports is today — focused on confidence, safety, and a genuine love for the ocean.
